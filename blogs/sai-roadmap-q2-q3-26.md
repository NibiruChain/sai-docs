# Sai Roadmap Q2 - Q3 '26

*For Q2-Q3, the focus continues to be around trader-first trading, frictionless funding, and a more powerful user experience.*

The priorities cluster around three themes: (1) shipping a native mobile app that becomes the primary surface for everyday traders, (2) integrating a fiat on-ramp so users can fund accounts directly with a credit or debit card, and (3) enhancing Sai's trading toolset so power users have full position control.

A native iOS and Android app. Most retail perp volume happens on mobile. The app will reach feature parity with the web experience and expose Sai's full trading surface in a mobile interface.

- Native iOS and Android builds with shared design system
- Full trading flow: markets, position management, deposits and withdrawals
- Push notifications for fills, liquidations, funding, and price alerts

Getting capital into Sai requires existing crypto, that typically lives onchain or off a centralized exchange. That's a hard stop for most new users. Integrating a fiat on-ramp provider with credit and debit card support enhances the funding flow into a few taps and unlocks a much larger top-of-funnel.

- Integrate a tier-1 on-ramp provider (e.g., Stripe Crypto, MoonPay) with multi-region coverage
- Credit and debit card funding directly into a user's Sai account. To also explore services that will enable Apple and Google Pay
- Embedded in both web and the new mobile app for a single funding experience

## Distribution Plan

High-level sequencing across the next two quarters. Dates are targets, not commitments; protocol upgrades and on-ramp integrations are prioritized so the mobile launch lands on a complete feature set.

| **Focus** | **Key Deliverables** |
| --- | --- |
| Platform UX Upgrades | Add margin to open positions; partial take-profit; reverse position; limit sell orders |
| Sai MCP | Ship Sai MCP server so traders & agents can interact with Sai programmatically (read markets, manage positions, place orders via Claude / agent stacks) |
| Sai Trading Bot | Plug-and-play integration with Telegram, Discord, and X so users can access Sai's engine through a dedicated interface inside the apps they already use; shared auth and account abstraction across surfaces |
| Fiat On-Ramp | Integrate fiat on-ramp provider; enable credit/debit card funding directly into Sai accounts; KYC flow + region gating |
| Multichain Trades | Deposit and trade from Base, Ethereum, and other EVM-compatible chains; one Sai account, many chains, no bridging required |
| Mobile App (Primary Focus) | iOS + Android native app: trading, portfolio, deposits, push notifications, biometric login; full feature parity with web |

## Forward-Facing Initiatives

The items above are prioritized for Q2–Q3. The initiatives below may be incorporated into the existing scope depending on bandwidth and capacity. The team continues to evaluate market conditions, and AI-based solutions are a growing focus that will keep moving up this list.

### New Products Inside the DEX

- Spot trading and swaps inside Sai
- Onchain lending: borrow against your collateral or lend out what's idle
- Social and copy trading: follow top traders, mirror their positions with one tap

### Interesting Financial Products

- Pre-launch token markets: take a position before a project's token goes live
- Structured notes: covered calls, principal-protected, yield-enhanced strategies as one-click products

### More Exotic Markets

- Equities, commodities, FX, rates onchain, 24/7
- Prediction and event markets: elections, sports, macro data, anything with a clear outcome
- Long-tail listings voted in by the community

### AI Agents and Strategy Managers

- Sai Co-pilot: an in-app assistant that suggests setups and explains risk
- AI Strategy Manager: give it a budget, a risk limit, and a thesis. It trades inside your parameters
- Natural-language trading: tell Sai what to do in plain English
- Agent-managed vaults: deposit into AI-run strategies with onchain performance
- Always-on risk monitor: watches positions, warns early, can hedge if you let it

---

Sai's near-term focus is removing friction for traders: a native mobile app, card funding, and the trade controls power users expect.

This roadmap will be dynamic. Priorities shift as the markets shift, and feedback from traders and partners directly influences what gets built and in what order.
