*Trade. Earn Points. Rise Through the Rankings.*

Sai Points rewards you for trading on Sai and contributing to the community. Every qualifying trade, completed bounty, and referral contributes to your points. The more you trade, the more you earn. Points convert to USDC at the end of the season.

If you already trade on Sai, this is how your activity starts working for you. If you're new, there's no better time to start.

## Timeline

Activity, PnL, and weekly streaks are tracked per season. A season lasts one month (four weeks). The points pool settles at season close.

## How it works

Points are cumulative per season. The points pool is distributed by your share of total points on the leaderboard for that season.

Earn for every dollar of volume. Boost your winnings by staying active and keeping your trading streak.

See below for how you can climb the points leaderboard.

## Season activity bonus...loyalty, loyalty, loyalty

Trading activity can accrue season multipliers for reaching per-week tiers or targets. For example, trading just $7.5K in volume in any week adds a global 2.5% multiplier to your season points. Trading $1M in volume increases the bonus to 10%. These multipliers stack and can be earned every week.

![Weekly tier bonus activity](/assets/sai-blog/09/02-tier-weekly.png)

Example: Trading $1M in volume every week of the 30-day season would result in a 40% increase on all points earned.

Worked example calculation: A trader trades in weeks 1, 2, and 4 and qualifies for different volume tiers.

```text
W1: 10K volume (Normie) --> +2.5%
W2: 55K volume (Degen) --> +5%
W3: 0 volume (No tier bonus) --> 0.0%
W4: 1M volume (Saicho) --> +10%

Total volume = 1,065,000 volume
Tier bonus = 2.5% + 5% + 0.0% + 10% = 17.5%

Total points = 1,065,000 volume * 1.175 (tier bonus)
= 1,251,375 points
```
